I am a B.Sc. in Computer Science student at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/), driven by a deep interest in *Programming Languages*, particularly *Type Systems* and *Language Features*.

I am currently undertaking research under the supervision of Prof. Lauren Pick on *Autoformalisation of Programming Language Theorems* as my Bachelor's thesis, whilst also collaborating on a project focused on specification generation to support agent-driven code synthesis.

During my academic exchange at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/), I joined the HScala research group under Prof. Philipp Haller. Our research focuses on designing a type system for heterogeneous [Scala](https://www.scala-lang.org/) execution across native CPU and SPIR-V-targeted GPU runtimes using *Capture Tracking*, as well as developing the initial compiler prototype.
