I am a B.Sc. in Computer Science student at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/), driven by a deep interest in _Programming Languages_, particularly _Type Systems_ and _Language Features_.

I am currently undertaking research under the supervision of [Prof. Lauren Pick](https://lmpick.github.io/) on _Autoformalisation of Programming Language Theorems_ as my Bachelor's thesis, whilst also collaborating on a project focused on specification generation to support agent-driven code synthesis.

During my academic exchange at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/), I joined the HScala research group under [Prof. Philipp Haller](https://www.phaller.com/). Our research focuses on designing a type system for heterogeneous [Scala](https://www.scala-lang.org/) execution across native CPU and SPIR-V-targeted GPU runtimes using _Capture Tracking_, as well as developing the initial compiler prototype.
